## 🧭 Prologue — A Different Kind of Tomb

There's a particular silence inside a tomb. Not the absence of sound — the *presence* of it. Dust suspended in a shaft of light. A lever that hasn't moved in four thousand years. And a player, sitting cross-legged on a carpet at 2 AM, wondering if they should just look up where the third Midas seal is.

**TombRaider2UltimateCheat** is what happens when someone decides the answer should be *no*. Not out of pride, but out of respect — for the puzzle, for the pacing, for the weird little rituals of the original 1997 experience.

This isn't a shortcut. It's a **companion**. A librarian, a cartographer, a stagehand who quietly nudges the spotlight when you're not looking. It's a trainer in the theatrical sense — the person backstage who hands you the prop so you can walk out and deliver the line.

If the classic *Tomb Raider II* experience is a cathedral, this project is the **candle rack** along the wall. You can walk in without it. But the shadows are warmer with it lit.

## 🎯 Philosophy of the Project

Most enhancement tools for classic games operate on a simple premise: *more is better*. More health, more ammo, more everything, immediately, until the game becomes a screensaver with menus.

We took the opposite path.

**TombRaider2UltimateCheat** is built around a word we like: **assistance**. Not replacement. Not domination. Assistance — the kind of thing a good co-op partner offers. When you slip on a ledge, they drop a rope. When you walk past a switch, they tap you on the shoulder. When you're stuck in the Opera House for forty minutes because you didn't realize the chandelier was climbable, they suggest, gently, that maybe you look up.

That's the design north star:

> *Enhance the player's agency. Never erase the player's presence.*

Everything in this repository flows from that sentence. The modules, the UI, the language files, the support tickets, the issue templates, the roadmap — all of it points at the same direction: **Lara keeps raiding. You keep playing. The experience becomes a better version of itself.**

We also believe strongly that software should feel *crafted*. Not generated. Not spammed. Every button has a reason. Every toggle has a sentence explaining what it does. Every error message reads like a human wrote it, because a human did.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

Below is the current constellation of capabilities. Think of these as stars — each one lights a different part of the sky, and together they form the shape of the suite.

### Core Trainer Modules

- 🩺 **Vitality Co-Pilot** — Adjust Lara's resilience with granular sliders rather than blunt on/off toggles. Tune the encounter difficulty to taste in real time, without editing memory by hand.
- 🎒 **Inventory Concierge** — Manage ammo pools, medkit supply, and flare reserves from a single panel. Changes apply live and are non-destructive to your save unless you ask them to be.
- 🗝️ **Key & Artifact Ledger** — View every collectible currently held in a clean, sortable ledger. Spawn keys you've already discovered elsewhere in the level for those "I know I had this" moments.
- 🧱 **Geometry Whisperer** — A noclip-style traversal aid rendered with a translucent overlay so you always know where solid ground is. Useful for level designers, speedrunners, and the chronicly lost alike.
- ⚖️ **Gravity Dial** — Low-gravity, high-gravity, or that cinematic slow-float mode. Adjusts in increments, not cliffs.
- 🕰️ **Time Sculptor** — Game-speed control from 0.25× to 4×, with a "bullet-time" preset for those last-second gap jumps.
- 🎯 **Precision Aim Assistant** — A soft-targeting layer that respects player input first and only nudges when you're clearly off. Fully disable-able, and off by default.
- 🧊 **Freeze Frame** — Pause the simulation without pausing the world. Rotate the camera, study a puzzle layout, then resume as if nothing happened.

### Quality-of-Life Enhancements

- 💾 **Save Slot Vault** — Snapshot your current progress into named slots independent of the game's native system. Roll back to any snapshot at any time.
- 🧭 **Waypoint Beacon** — Set a soft waypoint in the world and watch a subtle compass ribbon bleed in at the edge of your screen. No map spoilers — direction only.
- 📸 **Cinematic Snapshot Mode** — Free camera, depth-of-field, film grain. Because some of these environments deserve a wallpaper.
- 🎞️ **Replay Ribbon** — Records the last thirty seconds of gameplay to a temporary buffer. Missed a cool jump? Rewind, watch, celebrate.
- 🔔 **Ambient Notifier** — Soft chimes for secret discoveries, save reminders, and idle time, all configurable or mutable.
- 🧹 **Session Janitor** — Cleans up temporary files and logs between play sessions, keeping your folders tidy without any manual housekeeping.

### 🎨 Responsive & Adaptive UI 🎨

The interface is not a dialog box glued onto the side of the screen. It's a **fluid surface** that responds to how you actually play.

- **Fluid Grid Layout** — Panels reorganize themselves based on window size. Compact 720p? Everything tucks into tabbed ribbons. 4K ultrawide? The full canvas unfurls beautifully.
- **Theme Engine** — Light, dark, high-contrast, "Temple" (warm sepia), and "Crypt" (cool slate) presets ship in the box. Custom themes are JSON files away.
- **Input Agnostic** — Designed primarily for keyboard and mouse, but fully navigable with a controller via the standard XInput bridge.
- **Density Modes** — Cozy, Balanced, and Dense — because some of us like information at a glance and others prefer breathing room.
- **Live Motion Comfort Settings** — Reduce, disable, or replace animations. Includes a dedicated mode for players sensitive to rapid UI movement.
- **DPI-Aware Scaling** — Automatically adapts on multi-monitor setups with wildly different pixel densities.

## 🗺️ Getting Started — A Gentle Orientation

We've deliberately kept setup out of the way. There's no terminal ritual. No wall of commands. The whole thing is designed to be opened, oriented, and understood in under three minutes.

1. **Obtain the suite** using the acquisition macro below.
2. **Unpack** it into a folder of your choosing — a place you'll actually remember, like a shelf.
3. **Launch the host application.** First-run will walk you through a short orientation tour.
4. **Point the suite at your game installation** using the friendly folder picker in the Settings tab.
5. **Pick a profile** — *Purist*, *Cinematic*, *Explorer*, or *Custom*.
6. **Play.** Everything else is optional.

The first-run orientation is skippable. The settings are re-openable. Nothing here is one-way. That's a design rule.

## 🎛️ Module Deep Dive

### 🩺 Vitality Co-Pilot

The most-used module, and the most-abused in lesser tools. Ours avoids the trap by offering **gradients**. Want to feel like a fragile archaeologist in one level and an unstoppable force in the next? You can, without ever leaving the game.

- Real-time adjustment with a lockable "floor" so you can't accidentally set yourself below a survivable threshold.
- Encounter-aware: some traps and falls will still be lethal if you set them to be. Choice is preserved.
- Presets bundled: *Purist*, *Wounded*, *Veteran*, *Tank*.

### 🎒 Inventory Concierge

A ledger, not a grenade launcher. Adjust counts, cap them, restore them, or empty them entirely.

- Sortable columns: type, count, last-modified, first-acquired level.
- Undo stack: any inventory change can be walked back within the session.
- Export to JSON for save-archivists who like to keep receipts.

### 🧱 Geometry Whisperer

Traversal assistance rendered as a translucent geometry pass, so you always know what you're standing on and what's actually air.

- Color-coded surfaces: solid, climbable, water, hazard.
- Adjustable opacity and color palette — including deuteranopia and protanopia safe modes.
- Fully separate render target — never touches the game's own framebuffer.

### ⚖️ Gravity Dial

Six decades of game design have shown us one truth: gravity is the most expressive knob in a level designer's toolbox. Now it's in yours.

- Range: 0.1× to 5.0×, with soft detents at semantically interesting values.
- Presets for *Moon*, *Mars*, *Cinematic*, and *Lara Classic*.

### 🕰️ Time Sculptor

For when the world moves faster than your fingers.

- Range: 0.25× to 4.0×, with sub-step control.
- "Bullet-time" preset binds to a single key by default.
- Frame-pacing aware — time dilation stays smooth even on fixed-timestep engines.

### 🎯 Precision Aim Assistant

We built this module three times. The first two didn't respect the player enough. This one does.

- Default: **off**.
- When on: a soft target bubble that you can leave at any moment by moving the mouse.
- Full sensitivity curve exposed as a graph, editable in the advanced tab.

### 🧊 Freeze Frame

The function that has sold more copies of this suite than anything else. Pause the simulation. Rotate the camera. Study the room. Plan the jump. Resume.

- Free camera with adjustable FOV, exposure, and film grain.
- Bookmark named viewpoints and return to them later.
- Compatible with Snapshot Mode for the ultimate wallpapers.

## 🏗️ Architecture — How the Candle Rack is Built

We treat architecture as a form of hospitality. If someone opens this repository wanting to help, they should never feel lost in the dark.

**Layered modules** — every feature lives in its own small module with an explicit manifest. Adding a module does not require touching the core. Removing one does not break the others.

**Event bus, not global state** — modules communicate through a typed event bus. This keeps coupling low and makes modules testable in isolation.

**Manifest-driven locales** — language files self-describe their locale, fallback chain, and completion percentage. A half-translated locale is a first-class citizen, not an error.

**Snapshot-based persistence** — the suite stores state as serializable snapshots, human-readable and diffable. If you ever want to debug what the tool did, you can read it.

**Non-invasive by default** — when the suite interacts with the game, it prefers readable, reversible interactions over permanent writes. Every write has a corresponding undo path documented in the module manifest.

**Bring-your-own-log** — logs are plain text, timestamped, and kept rotated by the Session Janitor. They are the first thing support asks for, and the first thing the Janitor tidies.

**Testing philosophy** — automated tests cover module boundaries, locale integrity, and persistence round-trips. Gameplay-adjacent modules are covered by an integration harness that runs against a synthetic fixture level.

**Style principles** — small files, clear names, comments that explain *why* rather than *what*. We do not write code as a puzzle for the reader.

## 🔁 Release Channels & Versioning Philosophy

We ship on three channels, and we name them after archaeological strata. Because of course we do.

- 🏺 **Surface Layer** — the stable channel. Everything here is polished, documented, and battle-tested.
- ⛏️ **Excavation** — the beta channel. Features that survived internal testing but want field verification.
- 🌋 **Bedrock** — the experimental channel. Unstable, occasionally strange, occasionally brilliant. Not for the faint of heart.

**Versioning** follows a `YEAR.MINOR.PATCH` scheme. The **2026** line is the current long-term support (LTS) branch and will receive fixes and locale updates until at least **mid-2027**.

Every release note is human-written. Every breaking change is called out at the top, not buried in a changelog appendix. We think that's just good manners.

## 🔐 Safety, Integrity & Responsible Enjoyment

There are two things this project cares about more than any feature: **the safety of your machine** and **the respect of your save games**.

- 🔒 **Signed release artifacts** — every published build carries a signature you can verify.
- 🧬 **Reproducible builds** — two developers building the same commit produce byte-identical artifacts.
- 🩹 **Save isolation** — no module writes to a save file without a pre-flight snapshot. Restores are one click.
- 🧯 **Panic key** — a single default hotkey immediately disables every active module, no questions asked.
- 📖 **Transparent logging** — every action the suite takes on behalf of the player is written to a rotating log.
- 🧑‍⚖️ **Code of conduct** — the community spaces are moderated. Racism, harassment, and gatekeeping are removed without ceremony.

We believe that a companion suite is a **guest in your house**. Guests are polite. Guests take their shoes off. Guests don't rearrange the furniture.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this compatible with the modern remasters?**
Yes — a dedicated compatibility layer handles TR2 content in the 2024 remastered collection. Menus differ slightly on that layer, and a few of the more esoteric modules are disabled there for technical reasons. The FAQ in the knowledge base has the full grid.

**Will this affect my achievements or save integrity?**
Achievements are evaluated by the game itself. Our save isolation policy means any modification is snapshotted and reversible. If you're chasing a specific achievement, we recommend the *Purist* profile.

**Why is the suite so large for what it does?**
Because roughly half of what ships is language data, integration tests, and the interactive orientation tour. You can trim the suite with the Slim Profile installer if you'd prefer a smaller footprint.

**Can I use this with my own custom levels?**
Absolutely. Community levels are one of the best things about this scene, and the Geometry Whisperer was practically designed with them in mind.

**Where do I report a bug or request a feature?**
The in-app ticket composer is the fastest path, because it captures your environment automatically. The public issue tracker is equally welcome and thoroughly read.

**Who maintains this?**
A rotating cast of volunteers who love classic games, clean code, and the specific joy of a well-placed lever. No usernames are listed here by design — everyone contributes equally, and no single name should overshadow the work.
